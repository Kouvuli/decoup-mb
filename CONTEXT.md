# DecoUp domain language

DecoUp is a marketplace for buying and selling new or used decor, furniture, and PC-related items, plus a lead-generation platform connecting Customers with independent home-decoration Service Providers. PC-assembly services are a planned later capability. Shipping and contextual chat support these core journeys rather than operating as standalone marketplaces.

DecoUp is intended for worldwide use. The initial launch market is Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam; later geographic expansion must preserve the same domain language while adapting approved regional policy. Mobile is the primary buyer, seller, and service-provider experience; the web app supports the same accounts and business rules, with public landing pages for marketing and discovery.

## Participants

**User account**:
One authenticated identity that can act as Buyer or Customer by default and may separately activate Seller and Service Provider profiles.
_Avoid_: A separate login for each marketplace or service role.

**Seller profile**:
The independently activated selling capability and public history owned by a User Account.
_Avoid_: Service Provider Profile or a second User Account.

**Service provider profile**:
The independently activated service-provision capability and public history owned by a User Account. Its verification and reputation remain separate from any Seller Profile owned by the same User.
_Avoid_: Seller Profile or a second User Account.

**Buyer**:
A person who purchases an item offered through the marketplace.
_Avoid_: Customer when the marketplace role matters.

**Customer**:
A person who submits a Service Request and may hire the selected Service Provider outside DecoUp.
_Avoid_: Buyer when the service role matters.

**Seller**:
An individual or verified business that offers new or used items. DecoUp operates the marketplace and does not initially own the inventory.
_Avoid_: Vendor as a generic synonym.

**Service provider**:
An independent professional or verified company that publishes Service Offerings and may accept qualified Service Leads. The Provider contracts and receives payment outside DecoUp at launch.
_Avoid_: Seller when the participant is offering work rather than an item.

## Marketplace

**Listing**:
A fixed-price offer for a new or used decor, furniture, or PC-related item. A listing may accept price offers; used listings normally represent one item, while verified businesses may offer stock quantities.
_Avoid_: Product when referring specifically to the seller's offer.

**Price offer**:
A buyer's proposed purchase price for a listing that accepts negotiation.
_Avoid_: Auction bid; DecoUp does not run listing auctions at launch.

**Order**:
A purchase of marketplace items from one Seller. The initial checkout creates one Order for one Seller.
_Avoid_: Booking when referring to buying an item.

## Services

**Service offering**:
A Service Provider's offer of work using a fixed package, assumption-based range, or quote-required pricing. Home-decoration work is in initial scope; PC assembly is planned later.
_Avoid_: Listing, bare starting price, or open-to-offers.

**Service request**:
A Customer's request sent to one selected Service Provider, including category, location, scope, timing, budget range, and optional images.
_Avoid_: Booking or open provider bidding.

**Service lead**:
A qualified Service Request accepted by its selected Provider. Acceptance consumes plan allowance and permits contact exchange between the matched parties.
_Avoid_: Booking, protected transaction, or a publicly visible contact request.

**Lead outcome**:
The reported result of a Service Lead: contact shared, hired, not hired, completed, or cancelled. It does not prove payment or create DecoUp transaction protection.
_Avoid_: Booking status or payment status.

**Provider plan**:
The Free or Pro entitlement controlling lead-response capacity and Provider tools. Pro may include clearly labelled sponsored placement but never hidden organic ranking advantage or guaranteed customers.
_Avoid_: Service completion commission or Premium plan at launch.

## Supporting journeys

**Shipment**:
Delivery or pickup of physical marketplace items, fulfilled by an integrated external shipping provider or arranged by the seller.
_Avoid_: Booking or a standalone transport job.

**Payment**:
A transfer associated with a marketplace Order or Provider Pro subscription. Service-work payment occurs outside DecoUp at launch.
_Avoid_: Order, Service Lead, or external service agreement.

**Commission**:
A disclosed fee earned by DecoUp after a marketplace Order completes successfully.
_Avoid_: Provider subscription, Listing fee, or advertising charge.

**Promoted placement**:
An optional, clearly labelled paid visibility boost for an eligible Listing or Service Offering. A Pro Provider may receive sponsored placement at launch; payment never guarantees organic ranking.
_Avoid_: Display advertisement or hidden ranking advantage.

**Display advertisement**:
Paid promotional content shown on DecoUp pages for a third party. It is outside the launch scope.
_Avoid_: Promoted placement for a DecoUp Listing or Service Offering.

**Chat**:
A contextual conversation between Buyer and Seller or between Customer and Service Provider, attached to a Listing, Order, Service Offering, Service Request, or accepted Service Lead.
_Avoid_: Support conversation, or an unrelated general-purpose message thread.

**Support conversation**:
A conversation between a participant and DecoUp support.
_Avoid_: Chat when referring to marketplace or service participants.

**Review**:
A post-completion evaluation tied to an eligible Order item or completed DecoUp-originated Service Lead, with one-to-five stars and optional text/images. Service Reviews prove lead origin, not payment. Marketplace and service Reviews remain separate; Customers do not receive public ratings at launch.
_Avoid_: Testimonial or unverified profile comment.
